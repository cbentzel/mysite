I rebuilt this site's front page after being envious of the reading experience on [Tufte CSS](https://edwardtufte.github.io/tufte-css/) for a long time. Narrow columns, serif type, and notes that live quietly in the margin instead of interrupting the text.

This is the first post published through the new pipeline — plain Markdown files in `posts/`, parsed with `gray-matter` and rendered with `remark`. More to come.
